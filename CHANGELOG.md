## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **`coaches-corner.html` · Team talk timers** — each talk framework (pre-game 3:00, timeout 1:00, between sets 3:00, post-game 5:00) gets a timeline: numbered segments sized by how long each step should take, start–end times on every step, and a real-time timer (Start / Pause / Resume / Reset) whose playhead lights the current segment and step and names it next to the clock.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Bench rules-of-thumb visuals** — all four cards now have a diagram. New: **Substitutions** (a serving specialist checks in at the sub zone and serves, a defensive specialist replaces a weak back-row passer, a taller blocker comes in to face their best hitter) and **Stats to track live** (three serves passed for a 3, a 2, and a 0, logged in a running pass-rating tally). **Timeouts** reuses the opponent-run scrimmage and **Communication** the serve-the-weak-passer diagram. The bench cards now use the same column width as the other card grids, so the controls fit.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Matchup court** — the game plan now opens with an "At the net" court diagram: one band per lane (left side, middle, right side) with our attackers and their blockers as chips colored by rating, an arrow for our attack and one for theirs colored by who has the edge (Big edge → Way behind). It redraws as soon as any rating changes, and screen readers get a lane-by-lane summary.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Situational playbook visuals** — all 14 Coach's Corner situation cards now have an animated, steppable diagram. New: opponent runs and timeouts, a flat team, late-set leads and deficits (live-score scrimmages), shrinking a targeted passer's area, shifting the block toward their big hitter, blocking the front-row setter's dump, deep serving targets with margin, and starting set 2 one rotation later so the best blocker faces their best hitter. The rest reuse the matching stacking, double-block shots, quick-attack, out-of-system, and rotational-defense diagrams. Engine: `receiveViz` supports per-passer areas, a highlighted weak passer, and a custom legend.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Visuals on every card** — every drill, plyometric, and strategy card now has an animated, steppable visual.
  - **Plyometrics (all 32).** Side views for jumps, bounds, hurdle and depth jumps, the box exercises, split jumps, and the push-up. Front views for lateral and block work: line hops, skaters, lateral hurdles, block-jump series, and lateral-to-vertical. Every med-ball throw shows the ball's flight, to a wall, the floor, or behind the athlete.
  - **Drills (all 32).** Court diagrams for serve receive, serving targets and pressure serving, setter release, out-of-system sets, hitting lines, line vs cross, shots vs the block, block footwork, read block, box digging, pursuit, pepper, coverage, free-ball transition, King of the Court, short court, and the shuffle circuit. All 12 game-pressure drills are scrimmages with a live scoreboard: sideouts, digs and transition, aces, serve errors, unforced errors worth 2, and coach free balls.
  - **Strategies.** Stacking, read / commit / bunch-spread / swing blocking, the four serving targets (weak passer, seams, deep zone 1, short at hitters), attacking the setter, tooling the block, and quick and back-row attacks.
  - **Engine.** Shared `plyoPose()` body positions (side and front views, mirrorable), plus `scrimViz`, `serveStratViz`, and `blockScheme` builders. Court visuals can now have no ball.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Technique visuals** — every Attacking → Technique card is now animated. **From above** (amber hips over white shoulders + hitting arm): twist before the jump vs twist after the jump (same blocker and line/cross lanes, so the disguise difference is visible) and torso coil → uncoil (hips → torso → shoulder → arm). **From behind the hitter** with a clock face: arm angle at 1, 12, and 3 o'clock. **Close-up** of forearm, hand, and ball: wrist snap over the top for topspin. **Side view** with a blocker: roll shot lobbing over the hands. Engine: figures can define their own skeleton (bones, joint dots, balls), balls can follow their own flight path, optional floor and head.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Attacking tab** — new tab with three sections. **By position**: high ball, go, and pipe (outside); quick, slide, and 31 (middle); right-side high ball and back-row D (opposite), each with an animated diagram of the approach and set that shows whether their middle blocker arrives before contact. **Vs the block**: no block, one, two, and three blockers, with the block's shadow shaded and each open shot (line, cross, sharp, tool, roll, tip, wipe) drawn and played in turn. **Technique**: reach to hit and neutral hitting arm / bow-and-arrow (animated side views), plus twist before vs after the jump, torso coil, wrist angle, arm angle, and roll shot. Every card has use-when, cues, common mistakes, and links to practice drills. Engine: guide-arm support for figures, taller figure frames, ball-only fades.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Serve-receive diagrams** — animated **3-person**, **2-person**, and **W (5-person)** formations in Strategies → Serve receive. Each passer's area of responsibility is a shaded circle (fewer passers → bigger areas; seams where circles meet); three serves per formation (at a passer, into a seam — the passer moving toward the target takes it — and to a weak spot) show who moves, the pass to target, and the setter's release. Court mode gains per-player colors.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Offense diagrams** — animated **5-1**, **6-2**, and **4-2** in Strategies → Offensive systems: serve → pass → setter to target → front-row hitters approach (dashed lanes + "N front-row hitters" count) → set → attack. 5-1 shows two rotations (setter back row: 3 hitters; setter front row: 2 + dump), 6-2 shows the front-row setter (S2) hitting right side, 4-2 shows the front-row setter with 2 hitters. Court mode gains scene cuts (fade between rotations / at the loop seam) and multiple timed highlights.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Step through visuals** — every animated visual (plyometrics, drills, defenses) gets a clickable phase track (one segment per phase, sized by duration, showing progress), ◀ / ▶ step buttons, and keyboard control (← / → to step, Space to play/pause when the visual has focus). Stepping pauses on the end of each phase — the position the movement arrives at (landing stuck, defense set, ball dug).
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Drill & strategy court diagrams** — top-down animated court mode for the visual engine (players with role labels that change as they rotate, ball flight with height/shadow, ball carried by a player, timed "open area" highlights). First diagrams: **Butterfly Passing** drill (serve → pass → target → follow your ball) and all four defensive systems in Strategies — **Perimeter**, **Rotational**, **Middle-up (6-up)**, **Man-back** (same opponent outside attack; shows each system's positions, the dig, and the area it leaves open).
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Exercise visuals** — looping side-view animated demos on the **Snap-Down** (rise → snap → 2 s stick with a knee-over-toes guide → reset) and **Box Drop & Stick** (step off a box → drop → two-foot stick → reset) and **Pogo Hops** (quick, stiff-ankle bounces with gravity-like easing) plyometric cards; supports props, a far-side leg, and fades; phase caption, pause/play, respects reduced motion, only animates while on screen. Data-driven `VISUALS` map so more drills/exercises can be added as pose keyframes.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Team Talks tab** — talk frameworks for pre-game, timeouts, between sets, and post-game; 27 situation cards (underdog/favorite, big games, opponent runs, losing composure, protecting a lead, tight late games, bad calls, wins, close/blowout/season-ending losses, one-on-one moments) with goal, example lines, what to avoid, and tone; moment + situation filters, search, copy-script; phrase bank and do/don't principles.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Game-pressure drills** — 12 new game-like drills (Start at 22–22, Down 21–24 comeback, Close It Out, Sideout or Sit, Run It: 3 Straight, Set-Point Serve (Iced), Fifth-Set Simulation, Errors Count Double, Momentum Swing, Coach's Curveball, Crowd Noise Scrimmage, Rotation Escape) under a new **Game pressure** filter; **pressure scenario dealer** (12 tight-game starting scores + random twist rule + stakes) with a rally-scoring **scoreboard** (serve tracking, set/match-point and deuce callouts, win-by-2, undo).
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Plyometrics tab** — 32-exercise library (landing & mechanics, vertical, horizontal, lateral, single-leg, reactive/depth, upper & total body) with intensity, level, equipment, volleyball transfer, cues, and easier/harder options; **session builder** (level × season phase × focus) that caps intensity, budgets foot contacts, and links each exercise to the library; safety & progression guidelines.
- **`coaches-corner.html`** — Coaching hub with three tabs: **Drills** (20 drills, skill/level filters, setup/flow/cues/progressions), **Strategies** (5-1/6-2/4-2, serve-receive patterns, perimeter/rotational/middle-up defense, blocking schemes, serving & attack strategy), **Coach's Corner** (matchup planner rating 7 positions strong/neutral/weak for both teams → lane-by-lane edges + game plan + practice-focus drill links; copy/print; ratings saved in `localStorage`; situational playbook + bench rules of thumb).
- **`tactical-board/`** — Static **3D tactical board & play visualizer** (ES modules + Three.js CDN): FIVB court, Team A/B pose silhouettes (setter / attacker / blocker / receiver), raycast drag, ball Bézier path handles, keyframes, timeline dock (play/scrub/speed), camera presets, JSON export/import, URL hash share; **`tactical-board/README.md`** with GitHub Pages deploy steps.
- `prototypes/09-setter-footwork.html` — Setter OOS / first-ball scenarios (in-system, tight, off-net, inside seam, wide pin); L/R foot keyframes + synced ball arc; phase strip, step badge, trail toggle; mobile framing.
- `prototypes/08-basic-drills.html` — Drill picker (pepper, wall ball, serve targets, passing ladder, solo wall); HUD checklists; floor rings / wall anchor; stylized ball loops; motion toggle; mobile-friendly HUD.
- **`docs/ideas.html`** — Renders **`IDEAS.md`** on **GitHub Pages** via `fetch` + **marked** (same-origin).
- **`docs/PRE_WRITE.md`** — Short pre-build checklist (IDEAS, plan, README).
- `prototypes/06-attack-sets.html` — Net zones 1–9, call-sheet sets, front/back-row attacks, optional ball animation.
- `prototypes/07-outside-hitter-approach.html` — OH approach vs **5 / 4 / Shoot**; dual feet + ball sync; mobile HUD + camera framing.

### Changed

- **`coaches-corner.html` · Print & keyboard polish** — **Print** now uses a light print palette (the dark-theme colors printed light-on-white) and the game plan's Print button prints only the matchup planner; visual controls, timers, and the tab bar are hidden on paper and cards don't split across pages. **Keyboard / screen readers:** visuals announce their step text only while paused (no stream of announcements from playing visuals), phase-track segments leave the tab order (use ← / → on the visual), and every button, summary, and link gets the amber focus ring.
- **`coaches-corner.html` · Visual labels fit on every screen** — checked all 121 visuals at phone width (375 px) and with reduced motion. Shot labels on the twist technique diagrams and the "high hands" tool label now sit inside the frame, the floor-line label on Line Hops and Zig-Zag Hops no longer touches the bottom edge, and the Lost-the-first-set legend is shorter.
